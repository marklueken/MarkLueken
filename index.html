About

Strategy. Security. Service.

I'm Mark Lueken, Chief Information Officer for the Illinois Army National Guard. I've spent over twenty years building IT and cybersecurity capability for organizations that can't afford to fail — from data centers in Springfield to distributed teams in Afghanistan.

I lead a red team of more than one hundred cybersecurity professionals at the annual Cyber Shield exercise, advise leadership on enterprise security and risk, and have directed the migration of major infrastructure to Azure Government Cloud. Outside the Guard, I run three businesses focused on bringing the same standards of precision and integrity to the private sector.
